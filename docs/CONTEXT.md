**Project Title:** Kinesis AI

**Team Name:** INOVE8  

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation
Across India and the developing world, millions of aspiring athletes, youth, and fitness enthusiasts train daily without access to certified athletic trainers or biomechanists. In competitive sports such as Football and Cricket, improper movement patterns lead to:
1. **Suboptimal Technique Development:** Bad habits ingrained during early grassroots training limit athletic ceiling.
2. **Preventable Musculoskeletal Injuries:** Unmonitored joint loading, asymmetric biomechanics, and fatigue-induced form deterioration cause ACL tears, lower back stress fractures, and tendonitis.
3. **High Cost Barrier:** Professional motion capture systems and sports academies remain unaffordable for grassroots talent.

---

## 2. Proposed Solution: Kinesis AI
Kinesis AI democratizes sports science by converting standard smartphone and laptop webcams into an AI movement intelligence platform.

### Innovation Highlights:
1. **Zero-Hardware Barrier:** Operates using single 2D RGB webcam video feed via real-time 33-point body landmark triangulation.
2. **Deterministic Real-Time Kinematics:** Instant angle, velocity, and symmetry computations running at 30 FPS without heavy server-side video streaming.
3. **Proactive Movement-Risk Engine:** Detects dangerous movement patterns before injury occurs with transparent athletic risk metrics.
4. **Local Privacy-First AI:** Leverages Ollama (`gemma3:4b`) for offline coaching and personalized planning, with Gemini 2.0 Flash as an intelligent fallback.
5. **Bilingual Accessibility:** Supports Hindi, Hinglish, and English voice interactions for nationwide inclusivity across urban and rural athletes.
